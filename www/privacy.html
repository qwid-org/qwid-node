Privacy

Last updated: 10 August 2026
Short version. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and makes no requests to any third party. Nothing is stored on your device, and nothing you do here is profiled or shared. The only record of your visit is a standard web-server access log.

Who is responsible

QWID Foundation operates the website at qwid.org. For any question about this notice or about your data, write to , or use the contact form.

Cookies and local storage

None are used. The site stores nothing on your device — no cookies, no localStorage, no sessionStorage, no fingerprinting. Because nothing is stored and nothing non-essential is loaded, no consent banner is required and none is shown.

Google Analytics was previously embedded on this site and was removed in August 2026, together with the cookies it set. No analytics product replaced it.

Third-party requests

There are none. Fonts and the one JavaScript library the audit page uses are served from qwid.org itself rather than from a content-delivery network, so loading a page never discloses your IP address to another company.

One request does leave the page: the live network figures in the header (block height, supply, validators) are fetched from explorer.qwid.org. That is our own server, not another company's, and it logs requests on the same terms as described below. If you block scripts, the figures simply do not appear and nothing else changes.

Links that leave the site — GitHub, LinkedIn, X — are ordinary links. Nothing is sent to those services unless you click through, at which point their own privacy terms apply.

Server logs

The web server records each request, as web servers do. This is the only personal data processed here.

WhatWhyKept for
IP address, timestamp, requested URL, HTTP status, user agent, referrer Operating the service securely: diagnosing faults and identifying abuse such as scanning or denial-of-service attempts Rotated daily, deleted after 14 days

The lawful basis is legitimate interest in keeping the site available and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are not used to build profiles, are not combined with any other source, and are not sold or shared. They are disclosed only if a competent authority compels it by law.

Blockchain data is not covered by this notice

The QWID network is a public blockchain. Addresses, transactions and balances are replicated across every node by design, and nothing published to a blockchain can be edited or erased — not by QWID Foundation and not by anyone else. This is a property of the technology, not a policy choice.

QWID Foundation does not operate a wallet service on your behalf and never receives your private keys or recovery phrase: they stay in your wallet, encrypted, and are never transmitted to the network. Consider carefully what you attach to an address, because it is permanent and public.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to the personal data held about you, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to or use the contact form.

In practice the only data held is the server log described above, which is identifiable solely by IP address — so a request needs to reach us within the 14-day retention window to be actionable.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), uodo.gov.pl.

Changes

If the site ever starts using analytics, embedding third-party content, or storing anything on your device, this page will be updated before that change goes live, and consent will be requested where the law requires it.
